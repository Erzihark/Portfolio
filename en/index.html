SERVICES

What I can solve for you

Four kinds of work. Tell me your problem and we decide together which one fits.

  • SRV · 01

    Internal systems and automation

    You feel it
    Your team spends the day between Excel, WhatsApp and paper, and orders or tasks get lost along the way.
    What I do
    I bring everything into one system made for you, and I automate the tasks that are done by hand today.
    Proof
    For an industrial staffing agency, the system ranks available workers by experience and distance for every request.

  • SRV · 02

    New apps and products

    You feel it
    You have an idea, or you want customers to buy and book from their phone, and you do not know where to start.
    What I do
    I help you define a simple first version and I build it for iOS, Android and the web.
    Proof
    For an education organization, I built one app with a single codebase for iOS, Android and web.

  • SRV · 03

    Improving existing software

    You feel it
    Your system or website is slow, it is getting old, and every small change takes weeks.
    What I do
    I find where it gets stuck, speed it up and update it piece by piece, without stopping your operation.
    Proof
    At a digital advertising company, pages went from loading in about 3 seconds to about 0.7.

  • SRV · 04

    Interactive experiences

    You feel it
    You want your brand to stand out, and you want people to touch, play and remember, not just look.
    What I do
    I create interactive content in 2D, 3D and augmented reality, and I measure how each visitor uses it.
    Proof
    Interactive ads with 2D, 3D, augmented reality and physics, with real-time tracking.

What I have already built

    Client · Industrial staffing agency in the US

    When a company asks for staff, the system ranks the available workers by experience, distance and fit for the job. The team finds the right person faster.

HOW I WORK

How I work

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Understand the need

    We talk about your business and what gets in your way.

  2. 02

    I explain what I would do, what it includes and what it costs, with a clear scope from the start.

  3. 03
